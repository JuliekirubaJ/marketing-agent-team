# Webinar speaker prerequisites: prep call checklist

Use this on the speaker prep or kickoff call, held **T-21 to T-18** (T-0 is the webinar day; every deadline means "no later than"). Cadence, channels and the email sequence come from `config/team.md` and `brand/channels.md`.

**Timeline anchor:** Email 1 (the generic invite) sends around T-10, so promotion-critical assets must land by **T-16**. Late assets skip design polish. With several webinars a month there is no buffer.

---

## 1. Topic, title, abstract and takeaways (promotion-critical)

| Discussion point | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Working title confirmed and **frozen**. It uses a canonical product name from `brand/glossary.md` and the house title pattern (for example "<PRODUCT>: <topic>"). Retitling after promotion starts breaks the landing page, emails and creatives. | T-16 |
| A 2-3 sentence abstract in the speaker's own words, written as an **outcome** ("You'll leave able to ___"), not an agenda. Marketing edits it for glossary compliance. | T-16 |
| 3-5 concrete takeaways ("what you'll learn" bullets): specific capabilities, not topics. These drive email subject lines, LinkedIn hooks and community-post framing. | T-16 |
| Which single takeaway is the **hero**, the one promotion leads with? Is there a hero moment (demo climax, before/after, surprising number)? | T-16 |
| What pain point does the session resolve, in the audience's words? This feeds subject lines and hooks, the highest-leverage 60 characters of the campaign. Get one alternate angle for the non-opener resend. | T-16 |
| Will there be a live demo, and of what? "Live demo: ___" measurably lifts registration, so call it out explicitly. | T-16 |
| Any preview or in-development features being shown? Flag them so promotion doesn't overcommit. Re-verify WIP items before any external mention. | T-16 |
| Does the topic connect to an approved tagline in `brand/positioning.md`? Confirm the attribution rules for any partner-attributed line. | T-16 |

## 2. Speaker identity and assets

| Discussion point | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Exact name spelling, current title, and company as it should appear publicly. | T-16 |
| Short bio (1-2 sentences, for email) and long bio (50-100 words, for the landing page), emphasizing credibility in the audience's ecosystem. | T-16 |
| Headshot: at least **1200x1200 px**, recent, front-facing, neutral or softly blurred background, uncompressed JPG/PNG (not a social thumbnail). This gates every promo asset and is the most common bottleneck. | T-16 |
| LinkedIn profile URL. Is it current, and is the speaker comfortable being tagged in company and advocacy posts? | T-16 |
| Co-speaker or external guest (partner, community expert, customer)? Who owns their bio, headshot and approvals? External guests add review lag, so collect their assets a week earlier. | T-21 |
| Do emails send "from" the speaker's name or the company sender? | Prep call |

## 3. Audience and level

| Discussion point | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Primary persona (pick ONE from `brand/audiences.md`). It sets the email lists, LinkedIn framing and which communities we seed (`brand/channels.md`). | T-18 |
| Who is it **not** for? This keeps the invite list clean and open rates honest. | T-18 |
| Content level 100/200/300: a business session (outcomes, cycle time, cost) or a technical one? Mixed sessions need their structure stated in the abstract. | T-18 |
| Prerequisites attendees should have (published on the landing page). | T-16 |
| Warm base (existing customers of related products, framed as an upgrade path that is gradual and never forced) or net-new organizations? Any segments to exclude or treat differently? | T-18 |

## 4. Session structure

| Discussion point | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Total duration (target 45-60 min) and the split, for example intro (5) / slides (10-15) / live demo (25-30) / Q&A (10) / CTA close (2-3). Demo-heavy is the norm: the slides frame it, the product is the hero. | T-14 |
| Agenda in 4-5 bullets (for the landing page and Email 1). | T-14 |
| A named **moderator** who reads questions, watches chat, keeps time and handles logistics, so the speaker never breaks flow. | T-14 |
| Q&A plan: live Q&A? Pre-submitted questions invited in the reminder email? An agreed phrase for parking questions we can't answer live. | T-7 |
| 1-2 audience polls (for example "What's your current tool for ___?"). Poll data feeds sales follow-up and post-event content. | T-7 |

## 5. Deck and visuals

| Discussion point | Deadline |
|---|---|
| The deck **must** be built on the master template (`config/paths.md`, deck template), sent at kickoff with the branded virtual background. Don't build elsewhere and "reskin later". Locked slides: intro/title, agenda, speaker intro, outro/CTA. Theme fonts and colours only, and don't move pre-placed logos. | Kickoff (T-21) |
| Slide count: about 1 content slide per 2 minutes of talk (45 min is about 15-18 slides; 60 min is 20-25 at most). No wall-of-text slides, and no statistic without a source line that matches `brand/claims-and-proof.md`. | T-14 outline |
| Deck review timeline: outline draft **T-14**, full content to marketing **T-7** (design polish plus a terminology, claims and pricing compliance pass), polished deck back **T-5** (48-hour speaker review), **locked T-2** (factual fixes only after lock). | T-7 / T-2 |
| Visual do's: real product UI screenshots (1080p, clean crops), data-forward visuals, diagrams redrawn in template style (sketches by T-7). | T-7 |
| Visual don'ts: no competitor logos or UI screenshots (comparisons are text or tables and go through brand review), no misuse of partner logos, no stock-photo cliches. | Prep call |
| Readability: body text 18pt+, titles 28pt+, high contrast only, and no meaning carried by colour alone. Run a back-of-room legibility test at the T-2 rehearsal. | T-2 |

## 6. Demo readiness and fallbacks

| Discussion point | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Demo environment: which tenant, workspace and dataset? A clean environment with **sample or synthetic data only**: no customer names, real financials or internal data anywhere, including workspace names, browser tabs, notifications and the taskbar. A dedicated browser profile with notifications off. | T-14 |
| Capture and present at **1920x1080 (16:9)** with 100% display scaling. Browser at 125%+ zoom during the demo, and 150%+ for planned detail moments. Enlarged cursor. | T-7 |
| Feature stability: is everything shown stable in the current build? List the preview-feature risks. | T-14 |
| **Backup demo recording** (MP4, 1080p) captured by T-3, plus an agreed moderator handoff signal if the live environment fails. Marketing can also cut the teaser clip from it. | T-3 |
| Capability and scale claims use the canonical names and figures in `brand/glossary.md` and `brand/claims-and-proof.md`. | T-7 |

## 7. Positioning guardrails (walk through these on the call: the highest-risk section)

Cover these verbally. Afterwards, send the glossary one-pager, have the speaker check their talk track against it, and run a marketing compliance pass too.

- **Company and product framing.** Use the framing in `brand/positioning.md` exactly, including how the product relates to any platform partner. Company-level traction numbers (customers, countries, years) are not product adoption numbers. Frame them the way `brand/claims-and-proof.md` says.
- **Pricing.** Use the canonical pricing terms in `brand/glossary.md` and never a deprecated one, even if it appears on old decks. Any illustrative cost figure always comes with its stated assumptions. Any figure attributed to a third party is cited with that attribution.
- **Competitors.** Comparisons from `brand/competitors.md` use directional or relative framing only, as text or tables, with no logos or UI screenshots. Implementation-speed claims are "positioned for", not "proven", until a customer reference backs them.
- **Partner ecosystem.** Lead with the partnership narrative in `brand/positioning.md`. Never frame anything as an attack on the partner's wider platform. Partner momentum stats are platform-level: quote them verbatim and don't paraphrase them into product claims.
- **Heritage and migration questions.** State the migration posture exactly as `brand/positioning.md` gives it (for example "gradual, never forced"). Don't conflate retired or legacy products with the current one.
- **"Who do I buy from?"** Answer from `brand/company.md` (who owns sales, billing, support, training). Verify the status of training and certification programs before promising dates.

## 8. CTA and next steps

| Discussion point | Deadline |
|---|---|
| ONE primary CTA at the close (demo booking, estimator, next-webinar registration, content download) and one secondary at most. It must match the follow-up email and post-event LinkedIn CTAs. Attendees and no-shows may get different CTAs. | T-14 |
| CTA destination live and tested (for example, cross-promote the next session in the series at the end of this one). | T-7 |
| Who delivers the CTA: the speaker on the closing slide, or the moderator? Script it either way. | T-7 |
| A personal follow-up channel ("reply to this email" or "message me on LinkedIn")? It materially lifts post-event conversion. | T-14 |

## 9. Logistics and technical

| Discussion point | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Date, start time and **timezone stated explicitly everywhere**. For an international audience, also show a second timezone. Check for clashes with partner events, holidays and our other webinars that week. **Frozen by T-3**: changes after the reminder email is drafted force a correction send. | T-21, frozen T-3 |
| Platform confirmed, registration link generated (one canonical URL; marketing manages UTMs; the base URL must not change after T-14), event listed on the upcoming-webinars page (`config/team.md`), landing page live by T-16. | T-16 |
| Join mechanics: unique per-registrant links or one shared link? Duration and Q&A split stated in the reminder email. | T-7 |
| **Recording consent in writing**: the session will be recorded and repurposed (on-demand page, YouTube, LinkedIn clips, blog). The same for guests. | T-14 |
| **Backup speaker named**, with the deck, demo access and talk track. | T-14 |
| Recording setup: 1080p camera at eye level, key light in front (never a window behind), external or wired mic (no laptop mic, no Bluetooth earbuds). Background: a tidy real room, then the approved branded virtual background, then soft blur. No personal or novelty backgrounds. | T-7 |
| **Tech check or dry run** on the actual platform, from the actual machine and room: audio, camera, screen share at 1080p, demo login, slide transitions, moderator handoffs. Wired internet for demo sessions. | T-3 to T-2 |
| Day-of: the speaker joins **30 minutes early** for a final check. Calendar hold confirmed. | T-0 |

## 10. Promotion involvement (the speaker as a channel)

| Discussion point | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Personal LinkedIn share: we draft a personal-voice post, the speaker edits and posts it, timed to the T-7 company post window. | Draft T-9, post T-7 |
| A 30-60 second teaser video (speaker on camera: "here's what I'll show you...") or permission to cut one from the backup demo recording. Used in the T-7 post and the T-1 reminder. | Record T-12 |
| Permission to quote the speaker in email copy and promo snippets. Approval workflow: the speaker approves anything in quotes or first person (24-48 hour SLA, two rounds at most; agree now whether silence means approval). Marketing owns the rest. | T-14 |
| Employee advocacy: 2-3 post variants circulate at T-7. Will the speaker comment on and reshare colleagues' posts? | T-7 |
| Community seeding at T-5 (persona-matched communities from `brand/channels.md`, value first). Is the speaker willing to answer technical replies in-thread under their own account? No hard selling. | T-5 onward |
| Reshare the T-1 "tomorrow" post and the T-0 "live in 2 hours" post. | T-1, T-0 |

## 11. Post-event commitments (decide BEFORE the event)

| Discussion point | Deadline |
|---|---|
| **Recording:** available when (same day, T+1, T+2) and where (gated page, YouTube, platform)? Approve the edited recording within 24 hours so the follow-up email ships at T+1. | Decide T-1; approve T+1 |
| **Slides:** shareable or not? Full or redacted (roadmap and pricing slides often come out)? Who produces the shareable version? | Decide T-1 |
| **Parked Q&A:** written answers within 48 hours. They feed the attendee nurture email, a Q&A blog post and community replies, all after a compliance pass. Assign someone to capture resources mentioned live. | T+2 |
| **Repurposing:** which 2-3 demo moments make the best 60-90 second clips (see the `webinar-snippets` skill)? A blog post drafted from the talk track (we draft, the speaker reviews). The companion page follows `docs/webinar-companion-page-spec.md`. | Clips T+2; companion page T+3; blog T+7 |
| A personal recap post ("thanks for joining: recording and top 3 questions"); we draft it. | T+1 to T+2 |
| A 15-minute sales and marketing debrief: hot questions, objections, competitors mentioned. It feeds the follow-up sequence and the post-mortem. | T+2 |
| Series continuity: does this session set up a sequel? If so, put the next registration link in the follow-up emails and on the recording page. | T+1 |

| T-0 | **Speaker joins 30 min early**; Email 3 (warm attendee); live-day post |

**One-line rule for the speaker:** content inputs about three weeks out, deck one week out, logistics frozen three days out, and follow-up decisions made *before* the event. Then the whole campaign runs without a single "quick question" chase email.
