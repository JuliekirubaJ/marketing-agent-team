# Win/loss analysis

## When to use
- Quarterly, to keep messaging and enablement honest
- After several losses to the same competitor or for the same reason
- Before a positioning refresh

## Inputs
Ask the user for:
- The deal set: won and lost deals in the period, with competitor, segment, deal size band and stage lost
- Any interview transcripts, call recordings, CRM loss reasons or survey answers
- Whether new buyer interviews can be run, and who will run them

Read: `brand/competitors.md`, `brand/positioning.md`, `brand/audiences.md`, `brand/claims-and-proof.md`.

## Steps
1. **Pick the sample.** Aim for a balance of wins and losses, across segments. Note the sample size; under ten deals, call the findings directional.
2. **Interview guide** (if new interviews are possible). Open questions, in order:
   - What was happening that made you look for a solution?
   - Who was involved in the decision, and what did each person care about?
   - Which options did you consider, and how did you narrow them down?
   - What made the final choice? What almost changed it?
   - What could the vendor you did not choose have done differently?
   Keep the interviewer neutral; ideally not the seller on the deal.
3. **Code the material.** For each deal, tag: trigger, decision criteria, competitor, deciding factor, objection, perception of price, perception of product fit, perception of the sales experience. Use the buyer's words, not CRM dropdown values, wherever possible.
4. **Find patterns.** Count how often each deciding factor appears in wins and in losses. Look for differences by segment and competitor.
5. **Separate fixable from structural.** Messaging, enablement and process gaps are fixable. Fit gaps (we are not right for this segment) should change targeting, not messaging.
6. **Write actions** with an owner agent for each.

## Output template
```
# Win/loss readout: <period>
Sample: <n won> / <n lost>, segments, sources | Confidence: <directional / solid>

## Headlines (3-5)
## Why we win (factor, count, example quote)
## Why we lose (factor, count, example quote, competitor)
## By segment / competitor (notable differences)
## Fixable vs structural
## Actions
| Action | Owner agent | Due |
(e.g. battlecard update -> sales-enablement-specialist; targeting change -> marketing-planner; message change -> product-marketer)
## Raw coding table (appendix)
```

## Quality checks
- Quotes are verbatim and anonymised unless the buyer agreed to be named.
- Counts are shown, so readers can judge weight. No percentages on tiny samples.
- Don't claim a process gap without evidence in the material.
- CRM loss reasons are treated as a starting point, not the answer.
- Plain, direct prose.
