# Award submission

## When to use
- An award deadline is within the next few weeks
- Building or reviewing the yearly awards calendar
- Someone forwards an award invitation and asks whether to enter

## Inputs
Ask the user for: the award name and link, the category, the deadline, the entry fee, and what they want to enter (product, customer project, team, person).

Read: `brand/company.md`, `brand/claims-and-proof.md`, `brand/positioning.md`, `brand/glossary.md`, and any existing recognition recorded in the brand files.

## Steps
1. **Decide whether to enter.** Score the award on:
   - Credibility: who judges, who has won before, do buyers recognise it?
   - Fit: does a category match what we can prove?
   - Cost: entry fee, any fee to use the winner logo, time to write
   - Use: could we use a win in sales material, on the website, in a release?
   Recommend enter, skip or watch. Be wary of awards where every entrant "wins" for a fee.
2. **Read the criteria closely.** List each judging criterion and its weight. The entry is structured around these, in their order, using their words.
3. **Gather evidence.** For each criterion: approved numbers from `brand/claims-and-proof.md`, customer stories cleared for public use, before-and-after results. Where a customer project is the entry, get the customer's permission first.
4. **Write the entry.** Lead each answer with the result, then how it was achieved. Respect the word limits exactly.
5. **Prepare supporting material:** images, short video, customer letter, as the award allows.
6. **Record the entry** in the awards calendar with the result date, and if it wins, hand to `pr-comms-manager` for the announcement.

## Output template
```
# Award: <name> | Category: <...> | Deadline: <date> | Fee: <...>
## Recommendation: enter / skip / watch, and why (4 lines)
## Criteria map
| Criterion | Weight | Our evidence | Source file |
## Entry text (one section per question, with word count / limit)
## Supporting material checklist
## Permissions needed (customers, partners, people named)
## After the result (announce plan if we win)
```

## Quality checks
- Every number and claim is approved, with its caveat where the format allows.
- Customer names only if cleared in `brand/company.md` or with written permission.
- Word limits respected.
- Plain, concrete prose. Judges read hundreds of entries; specific results stand out, adjectives do not.
- Canonical terms from `brand/glossary.md`.
