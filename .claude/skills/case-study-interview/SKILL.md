# Case-study interview

## When to use

- A customer has agreed to a case study, a quote, or a customer-speaker slot that needs a story.
- Sales has a strong win and wants it captured before the details fade.

## Inputs

Ask for: customer company (or anonymised descriptor), interviewee name and role, product and use case, what the account team already knows (outcome, timeline, numbers), naming status (named, anonymised, pending approval), and the target persona for the story.

Read: `brand/company.md` (products, nameable customers), `brand/audiences.md`, `brand/claims-and-proof.md`, `brand/glossary.md`.

## Steps

1. **Pre-call (from the account team):** what they did before, why they changed, what they chose, results so far. Write down the numbers you expect to confirm.
2. **Send the brief** to the interviewee: purpose, 30-45 minutes, topics (not the full question list), that it will be recorded for accuracy, and that they approve the draft before anything is published.
3. **Open the call:** confirm consent to record and how the company may be named.
4. **Ask in four parts.** Use open questions, follow the interesting answer, and ask "can you give me an example?" often.
   - *Before:* What did the process look like? Who was involved? What was it costing you in time, errors or missed decisions?
   - *Trigger:* What made you look for something else, and why then? What else did you consider?
   - *Change:* How did rollout go? What surprised you? What would you tell a peer starting now?
   - *After:* What is different now? How do you measure it? Which number would you put your name to?
5. **Pin down every figure:** the baseline, the new value, the time period, and how it was measured. "About half the time" becomes "from roughly 10 days to 5 days per cycle, measured over two quarters", or it stays qualitative.
6. **Close:** quotable line in their own words, permission for logo use, and who in their company approves.
7. **Within 24 hours:** fill in the notes template, mark each figure as confirmed or to verify, and send to content-writer.

## Output template

```
# Case-study notes: <customer or descriptor>
Interviewee: <role>   Date: ...   Recorded: yes / no   Naming: named / anonymised / pending

## Snapshot
Industry ... | Size ... | Product ... | Use case ...

## Before
## Trigger and alternatives considered
## Change (rollout, surprises)
## After
| Metric | Before | After | Period | How measured | Status (confirmed / to verify) |

## Quotes (verbatim, with timestamp)
## Approval path
## Gaps to follow up
```

## Quality checks

- Quotes are verbatim and timestamped; nothing is put in the customer's mouth.
- Every figure has a baseline, a period and a measurement method, or it is not used as a number.
- Product names follow `brand/glossary.md`, even if the customer used a different name.
- Nothing is published before the customer's written approval.
