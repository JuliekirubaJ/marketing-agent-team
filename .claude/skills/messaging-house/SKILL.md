# Messaging house

## When to use
- Before a launch, so every asset tells the same story
- When positioning changes or a new persona is added
- When campaign copy from different writers says different things
- As the source document for talk tracks, one-pagers and website copy

## Inputs
Ask the user for: the product or feature in scope, the audiences in scope, and any messaging they already like or dislike.

Read: `brand/positioning.md` (existing positioning, pillars and approved taglines), `brand/company.md`, `brand/audiences.md`, `brand/competitors.md`, `brand/claims-and-proof.md`, `brand/glossary.md`, `brand/voice-and-tone.md`.

If `brand/positioning.md` already has pillars, build on them. Do not invent a parallel set.

## Steps
1. **Write the positioning statement** (internal, not customer-facing): for whom, the problem, the category, the key benefit, the alternative they use today, and what makes us different.
2. **Write the umbrella message (the roof).** One sentence a customer would understand without context. Then a two-to-three sentence elaboration.
3. **Choose three or four pillars.** Each pillar is a distinct reason to believe. Test: could a competitor say the same thing truthfully? If yes, sharpen it or drop it.
4. **Fill each pillar** with a headline, a short explanation, the capabilities that deliver it, and proof points. Proof must come from `brand/claims-and-proof.md` with its caveat. Where proof is missing, write **[PROOF NEEDED]**.
5. **Lay the foundation:** the facts that support every pillar (partnerships, security, scale, company credibility), each traced to a brand file.
6. **Write persona versions.** For each persona in `brand/audiences.md`, adapt the roof and pick the pillar that matters most to them. Technical and economic buyers get separate versions.
7. **List words to use and avoid** for this message, consistent with `brand/glossary.md`.
8. **Propose additions** to `brand/positioning.md` for the owner to approve.

## Output template
```
# Messaging house: <product / feature>
## Positioning statement (internal)
## Roof: umbrella message + 2-3 sentence elaboration
## Pillars
| Pillar | Headline | What it means | Capabilities | Proof (claim + caveat + source) |
## Foundation (credibility facts + source files)
## By persona
| Persona | Lead pillar | Message | Primary objection | Answer |
## Words to use / words to avoid
## Gaps: proof needed, decisions needed
```

## Quality checks
- Approved taglines only, from `brand/positioning.md`, with their attribution.
- Every proof point traces to `brand/claims-and-proof.md`. No invented numbers.
- Pillars do not overlap, and each one passes the "could a competitor say this" test.
- Canonical terms from `brand/glossary.md`.
- The roof reads like something a person would say out loud.
