# Pricing page review

## When to use

- A pricing or plans page is being written or redesigned.
- Pricing-page traffic is healthy but demo requests, trials or purchases are low.
- A competitor changed its pricing presentation and someone asks how ours compares.

## Inputs

Ask for:
- The URL or a draft, plus screenshots on desktop and mobile.
- The goal of the page: self-serve purchase, trial start, or sales conversation.
- Visits, conversion rate and top exit links, if known.

Read `brand/company.md` (products and plans), `brand/positioning.md`, `brand/competitors.md` (pricing comparisons and their caveats), `brand/claims-and-proof.md`, `brand/glossary.md` and `brand/source-authority.md`.

## Steps

1. **Fact check first.** Every price, plan name, limit and included feature on the page must match the brand files. If the page and `brand/` disagree, use `brand/source-authority.md` to decide which wins and flag the conflict. Don't fix prices yourself.
2. **Can a buyer answer the four questions** in under a minute: what does it cost, what do I get, which plan is for me, what do I do next?
3. **Plan structure.** Plan names describe who they are for. The recommended plan is marked. Differences between plans are the few that matter, not every feature.
4. **Anchoring and framing.** Billing period stated clearly (monthly or annual, per user or per unit). Discounts shown honestly. Totals not hidden in footnotes.
5. **Comparison table.** Rows grouped by job to be done, not by internal module. Tooltips for jargon. Glossary terms used exactly.
6. **Cost of the alternative.** If `brand/competitors.md` has an approved comparison, it can appear with its caveat. Never state a competitor price without a source and date.
7. **Objections and FAQ.** Pull objections from `brand/audiences.md` and sales notes: contracts, licensing prerequisites, security, data residency, cancellation, support. Answer plainly.
8. **Paths.** A clear primary CTA per plan, a "talk to sales" path for large or complex buyers, and no dead ends.
9. **Trust.** Customer proof allowed by `brand/company.md`, security and compliance notes from `brand/claims-and-proof.md`.
10. **Mobile.** The plan table works without sideways scrolling or collapses sensibly.

## Output template

```
## Pricing page review - <page> (<date>)
Goal: ...   Data: <rates and dates, or "heuristic only">

### Fact check
| Item on page | Page says | brand/ says | Source file | Action |

### Findings
| # | Area | Finding | Impact | Effort | Fix or test |

### Suggested changes (copy drafts for copy-editor)
### FAQ additions
### Open questions for the owner
```

## Quality checks

- No price, discount or competitor figure that isn't in `brand/` with a source.
- Caveats from `brand/claims-and-proof.md` travel with the claim.
- Glossary terms and capitalisation exact.
- Plain language: no "unbeatable value" style hype.
