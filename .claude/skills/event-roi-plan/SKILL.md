# Event ROI plan

## When to use

- A sponsorship prospectus or event invite has arrived.
- The annual event calendar needs ranking.
- After an event, to compare actual results with the plan.

## Inputs

Ask for: event name, dates, location or format, expected attendance and audience mix, package options and prices, what each package includes (speaking slot, attendee list, booth size, meeting space), staff available, and the commit deadline.

Ask for, or label as assumptions: average deal size, meeting-to-opportunity rate, opportunity-to-win rate. Never invent these. If past event results exist, use them.

Read: `brand/audiences.md` (ICP and personas), `brand/competitors.md`, `config/team.md`.

## Steps

1. **Fit score (1-5 each):** share of attendees in the ICP; seniority match; whether buyers or only users attend; competitor presence; timing against our own campaigns; speaking or content opportunity.
2. **All-in cost:** package, booth build and graphics, shipping, travel and hotel per person, staff days (at an agreed day rate or as a count), swag, pre-event outreach, lead-capture tools.
3. **Expected outcomes:** realistic qualified meetings (booked in advance plus walk-ups), not badge scans.
4. **Break-even:** cost / (deal size x meeting-to-opportunity rate x win rate) = qualified meetings needed. Show the formula with the numbers.
5. **Non-pipeline value:** customer meetings, partner time, content captured, competitive intel. Note it, but do not let it carry a weak pipeline case on its own.
6. **Recommendation:** GO, NO-GO, or GO with a condition (smaller package, speaking slot only, attend without a booth).
7. **If GO:** the 3-5 things that must be true (meetings booked before arrival, staffing, lead capture tested).

## Output template

```
# <Event>: ROI plan
Recommendation: GO | NO-GO | GO if <condition>
All-in cost: <amount>   Break-even: <N> qualified meetings   Decision by: <date>

## Fit
| Factor | Score | Note |

## Cost
| Line | Amount |

## Break-even
<formula with numbers>
Assumptions: <each one labelled, with its source or "to confirm">

## Non-pipeline value
## Conditions for success
```

## Quality checks

- Every rate and deal size is sourced or labelled as an assumption.
- Cost is all-in, including people's time.
- Meetings, not badge scans, drive the case.
- Plain language; no inflated reach figures copied from the prospectus without saying so.
