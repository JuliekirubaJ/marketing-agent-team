# Co-marketing brief

## When to use
- Any joint program with a technology partner, platform owner, consultancy or reseller
- Before asking a partner for program funds or featured placement
- When a partner proposes a joint activity and the team needs to decide whether to do it

## Inputs
Ask the user for: the partner, the proposed activity, the goal, the budget on each side, the contacts on each side (by role), and the timeline.

Read: `brand/company.md` (partner list, relationship wording, what may be said publicly), `brand/positioning.md`, `brand/audiences.md`, `brand/claims-and-proof.md`, `brand/channels.md`, `brand/glossary.md`, `config/team.md`.

## Steps
1. **Check the partner is listed** in `brand/company.md` and note the exact relationship wording. If not listed, stop and ask the owner.
2. **Agree one shared goal** and how each side measures it. The partner's goal is often different (consumption, marketplace transactions, partner-attached revenue); write both down.
3. **Define the audience.** Which of our personas, which of theirs, and where they overlap.
4. **Write the joint value proposition** in one or two sentences: what the customer gets from the combination that neither gives alone.
5. **Choose the format and assets** (webinar, guide, event, campaign, listing promotion) and list every asset with an owner side.
6. **Split work and cost.** Who writes, who designs, who hosts, who pays for what.
7. **Agree lead handling.** Who owns registrations, consent language on forms, how and when leads are shared, and who follows up first.
8. **Set approvals.** Each side's reviewers, brand rules to follow, and time allowed per review round.
9. **Build the timeline** back from the go-live date, including partner review time.

## Output template
```
# Co-marketing brief: <PARTNER> x <COMPANY>: <activity>
Relationship wording (from brand/company.md): "<...>"

## Goal and measures (ours / theirs)
## Audience (our personas / their personas / overlap)
## Joint value proposition
## Format and assets
| Asset | Owner side | Owner role | Due |
## Work and cost split
## Lead handling (owner, consent text, sharing method, follow-up order)
## Approvals (reviewer role per side, rounds, days per round)
## Timeline
## Risks and open questions
```

## Quality checks
- Relationship wording is exact; no upgrading "partner" to "endorsed by".
- Consent language on any shared form covers both companies.
- No partner logos or quotes unless cleared.
- Only channels in use per `brand/channels.md`.
- Canonical terms from `brand/glossary.md` for our products; the partner's own naming for theirs.
